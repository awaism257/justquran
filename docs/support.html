About

JustQuran presents the Holy Quran verse by verse in the Digital Khatt IndoPak script, with the classic Urdu translation of Mawlana Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari and the ClearQuran English translation — together with transliteration, recitation, search, bookmarks, and a reading-friendly design in light and dark themes.

The app works fully offline: all texts and fonts are bundled, and recitation audio is downloaded only when you choose.
