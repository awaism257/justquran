Privacy Policy

JustQuran — effective 19 September 2026

In short: JustQuran collects nothing. No personal data, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking — in the Android app and on this website alike.

What stays on your device

  • Your bookmarks, last-read position, and settings (theme, font sizes, toggles) are stored only on your own device. They never leave it.
  • Downloaded recitation audio is stored on your device for offline listening.

What we collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • No usage statistics, crash analytics, or device identifiers.
  • No advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no ad networks.
  • No location data, no contacts, no files beyond the app's own data.

Network use

The app works fully offline. The only network requests it ever makes are direct file downloads you initiate yourself (recitation audio). No user data is attached to those requests. This website is served as static files by Netlify; please see Netlify's privacy policy for their standard server logs.

Children

JustQuran is a general-audience religious text app. It is not directed at children under 13 and, since it collects no data whatsoever, it processes no children's data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Please use the contact email address shown on the JustQuran Google Play store listing.
