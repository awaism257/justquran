JustQuran is a clean, modern, offline reader for the Holy Quran, presented in Arabic together with Urdu and English translations.

Texts and Scripts:
• Arabic: Digital Khatt IndoPak (QUL/CDT edition, paged layout) and Tanzil Uthmani (verse-by-verse)
• Urdu: Authentic translation by Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari
• English: The ClearQuran translation by Talal Itani
• Transliteration: English phonetic transliteration for every verse

Features:
• Folio / Paged Mushaf View: Centered IndoPak paged layout with waqaf symbols, surah headers, and bismillah markers
• Verse-by-verse Card View: Read Arabic, translation, and transliteration side by side with customizable visibility
• Audio Recitation & Narration: Listen to Arabic recitation (Mahmoud Khalil Al-Hussary / Ibrahim Walk) alongside English and Urdu audio narrations verse-by-verse, or listen continuously like an audiobook in the background
• Translation Books: Dedicated book view for English and Urdu translations with active verse narration highlighting
• Full-Text Search: Fast offline search across Arabic, English, and Urdu texts
• Navigation: Surah index, Juz index with quick filtering, bookmarks, and Khatm al-Quran supplication
• Offline & Private: No internet required for reading; optional offline audio downloads
• Appearance: Light and dark themes with independent font size controls for Arabic, Urdu, and English

JustQuran is completely free and open source (MIT license). It contains no advertisements, no subscriptions, no user tracking, and requires no account.
